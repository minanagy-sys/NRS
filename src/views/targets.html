05 — Target file

Export, edit, upload

One Excel workbook with two sheets you can edit: Branches and Doctors, October 2026 to December 2027. Change any number, add or delete a branch or doctor row, then upload the workbook. A blank cell keeps the number already in the dashboard. A branch or doctor row you delete is removed.

Accepted files. .xlsx with a sheet whose first column is Branch and/or a sheet whose first column is Doctor, or a single .csv of either. Month columns must be named like 2026-10. From Numbers, use File → Export To → Excel. The Services sheet in the export is for reference; it is rebuilt from the branch numbers and is ignored on upload.

How the numbers were built

  • Revenue. Posted customer invoices ex-VAT. 2024 to March 2026 from the journal exports, April to September 2026 from Odoo 18. Package Sale journal excluded.
  • Months. The average monthly shape of 2024 and 2025. Ramadan falls around February–March 2027, so February may need a manual edit.
  • 2027 branches. Each branch has a minimum 100% target per month: Chizar 4.0M, The Strip 4.2M, City Stars 4.03M, CFC 4.0M, Mall of Arabia 2.0M, Roshdy 1.5M, Zayed and Mohandseen 1.0M, El Rehab, Madinty and Loran 0.5M. The floor is the lowest the 100% target can be in any month: each month keeps the approved trend number, raised to the floor where the trend was lower. Golden Square keeps its ramp from April 2027. August 2026 (21.3M ex-VAT) is the reference: the weakest 2027 month is above it.
  • October–December 2026. The earlier bridge, with every branch raised to at least its floor. That gives 68.4M; the trend forecast of about 54M lands near 79%, just under the commission floor.
  • ZAT. Madinty, El Rehab and Golden Square are tagged ZAT and are inside the 300M, as in your file. Use the brand filter to see them apart.
  • Days. Branch days use the 2026 weekday pattern (Friday is about 60% of an average day). Doctor days follow her Odoo shifts, and doctors with no shift are spread evenly across the month.
  • Doctors. Your approved doctor file (62 people), moved with the branches each doctor works in: her target is split across her branches by shift hours in Odoo, each part grows with that branch, and the total equals the branches that have doctors on the schedule. People not in the file show actual revenue but no target.
